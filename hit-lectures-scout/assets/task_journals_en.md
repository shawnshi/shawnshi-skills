# International Medical and Digital Health Journal Search Reference

Use this file to design searches and record evidence. It is not a source and does not require a particular search tool.

## 1. Suggested primary sources

- Nature and Nature Medicine
- JAMA Network
- New England Journal of Medicine
- The Lancet
- BMJ
- PubMed, PubMed Central, trial registries, conference proceedings, and journal pages

Apply the user-specified date range. Record publication, online-first, version, and retrieval dates separately when they differ.

## 2. Suggested queries

- `"clinical AI" OR "large language model" OR "foundation model"`
- `"digital health" OR "electronic health record" OR "medical imaging"`
- Add the disease, care setting, workflow, study design, method, or system named in the request

## 3. Inclusion and appraisal

- Include clinical trials, observational studies, model evaluations, methods papers, systems research, datasets, and foundational work when relevant to the question.
- Do not require real-world evidence for every study. Assess external clinical validation only when it is applicable to the study's claim.
- Record N, P values, confidence intervals, effect sizes, and clinical endpoints only when applicable and reported by the source. Use `not applicable` or `not reported` otherwise.
- Do not discard a relevant methods or foundational paper because it lacks a commercial mapping.

## 4. Suggested record

```markdown
### [Original title / translated title if useful]
- **Source type and review status**: [peer-reviewed article/conference paper/preprint/methods paper]
- **Venue, date, and version**: [source metadata]
- **DOI or permanent link**: [verified locator]
- **Question and design**: [what was studied and how]
- **Population/data/baselines**: [when applicable]
- **Main result**: [source-supported result]
- **Uncertainty and limitations**: [bias, generalizability, calibration, replication, access gaps]
- **Relevance to the research question**: [neutral explanation]
- **Testable follow-up hypothesis**: [optional]
```

Return the number of studies that actually meet the criteria. Do not fill a quota with older, popular, or lower-quality material.
