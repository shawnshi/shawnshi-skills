# anti-gravity

Layered spatial composition with floating objects, restrained depth and a technology-artifact feel.

## Design aesthetic

Treat the slide as a quiet three-dimensional field. Cards, diagrams and product objects appear to float at different depths, but reading order remains explicit. Use the effect to reveal relationships, not to decorate every page.

## Background

- Deep navy `#07111F` or near-black `#090B10`.
- Optional soft radial light behind the main object.
- Avoid busy star fields, fake particles or excessive glow.

## Typography

- Headline: geometric sans-serif, bold, large, short line length.
- Body: neutral sans-serif with generous line spacing.
- Labels: medium-weight sans-serif; use monospace only for technical identifiers.
- Keep CJK fallback fonts available and preserve glyph legibility.

## Color palette

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#07111F` | Main field |
| Primary text | `#F8FAFC` | Headlines and body |
| Secondary text | `#94A3B8` | Labels and metadata |
| Electric blue | `#38BDF8` | Primary relationship |
| Violet | `#8B5CF6` | Secondary depth layer |
| Mint | `#5EEAD4` | Positive or active state |
| Amber | `#F59E0B` | Risk or attention |

## Visual elements

- Floating panels with consistent perspective and restrained shadow.
- Thin connector lines showing true dependencies.
- Semi-transparent depth planes for architecture or layered concepts.
- One hero object or diagram per slide.
- Subtle motion cues may be used in the editable deck, but the static frame must remain understandable.

## Layout rules

- Keep a stable grid beneath the spatial effect.
- Use no more than three depth planes on a standard content page.
- Place the headline and takeaway on a flat, high-contrast plane.
- Keep citations, confidentiality markers and necessary labels in a stable footer or source-note zone.
- Do not tilt dense tables or long paragraphs into perspective.

## Assets

- Prefer owned or licensed product renders, diagrams and abstract objects.
- Browser or app screenshots are optional. Use them only when authorized and appropriately deidentified; record rights and deidentification in the Assets field.
- Preserve authorized brand elements when required by `Template_Ref` or `Brand_Rules`.

## Avoid

- Unreadable glassmorphism, excessive blur or neon outlines.
- Decorative floating objects without information value.
- Perspective that distorts numbers, charts or citations.
- Removing required citations, confidentiality markers or authorized brand elements for visual purity.

## Best for

Technology keynotes, innovation narratives, product launches, AI platforms and conceptual system presentations.
