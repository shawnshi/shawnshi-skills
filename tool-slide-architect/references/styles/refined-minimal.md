# refined-minimal

Quiet editorial minimalism with precise spacing, restrained color and high-quality typography.

## Design aesthetic

Use whitespace and proportion to establish authority. Each slide has one focal point and only the supporting detail required to understand it. Minimal does not mean omitting evidence, limitations or necessary labels.

## Background

- Warm white `#FAFAF7` or pure white `#FFFFFF`.
- Optional near-black `#151515` section pages.
- No decorative texture unless the approved template requires it.

## Typography

- Headline: refined sans-serif or restrained serif, 28–40 pt depending on format.
- Body: neutral sans-serif, 16–22 pt for projected use.
- Metadata and citations: 9–12 pt with sufficient contrast.
- Use at most two font families and maintain reliable CJK fallback.

## Color palette

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#FAFAF7` | Main canvas |
| Primary text | `#171717` | Headlines and body |
| Secondary text | `#6B7280` | Metadata |
| Divider | `#D4D4D4` | Hairlines and grid |
| Accent blue | `#315EFB` | Primary emphasis |
| Accent clay | `#C96F4A` | Selective contrast |

## Visual elements

- Thin rules, generous margins and disciplined alignment.
- One chart, image, statement or comparison as the primary object.
- Small labels and direct annotations instead of decorative icons.
- Monochrome or low-saturation photography only when it adds evidence or context.

## Layout rules

- Use a consistent grid and stable title position.
- Prefer `minimal` or `balanced` density.
- Keep no more than one accent color dominant on a slide.
- Give data and quotations visible sources without competing with the main message.
- Logo grids may be used only for authorized logos; retain original marks unless the license allows recoloring.

## Avoid

- Empty space that forces essential context into unreadable notes.
- Thin gray text with insufficient contrast.
- Decorative icons, gratuitous cards or repeated dividers.
- Removing required citations, confidentiality markers or authorized brand elements.

## Best for

Executive briefings, strategy decks, portfolios, board materials, premium proposals and concise research summaries.
