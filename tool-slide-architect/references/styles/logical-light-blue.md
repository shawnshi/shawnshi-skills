# logical-light-blue

Light-blue systems architecture style combining precise hierarchy, restrained isometric depth and business readability.

## Design aesthetic

Use light technical backgrounds and blue depth cues to explain systems, responsibilities and evolution paths. Isometric treatment is optional and should clarify layers; dense text, charts and citations remain flat and readable.

## Background

- Light slate `#F1F5F9` to white `#FFFFFF`.
- Optional subtle grid `#E2E8F0` at low contrast.
- Use white panels to separate dense content from spatial diagrams.

## Typography

- Headline: modern sans-serif, bold, deep blue.
- Section labels: medium-weight sans-serif with moderate letter spacing.
- Body: neutral sans-serif in `#1E293B`; concise verb-led labels are preferred, but clear explanatory language is allowed.
- Notes and citations: readable neutral text; do not force tiny white text into colored blocks.

## Color palette

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#F1F5F9` | Main canvas |
| Primary text | `#1E293B` | Headline and body |
| Deep blue | `#1E3A8A` | Foundation layer |
| Core blue | `#3B82F6` | Service or logic layer |
| Light blue | `#93C5FD` | Experience layer |
| Amber | `#EAB308` | Risk or dependency |
| Red | `#DC2626` | Critical exception only |

## Visual elements

- Layered architecture plates, orthogonal flows and clear system boundaries.
- Consistent line weight and arrow direction.
- 2.5D or 30° isometric planes only when depth represents a real layer.
- Flat charts, tables and decision cards alongside diagrams.
- Direct labels and concise callouts connected with orthogonal or simple straight leaders.

## Layout rules

- Use a 12-column or equivalent grid for page alignment.
- Keep diagram depth to three or four meaningful layers.
- Use color consistently for foundation, core and experience layers.
- Preserve visible citations, confidentiality markers and authorized brand elements.
- Real system screenshots are optional. Use only authorized, appropriately deidentified interfaces and record their status in Assets.

## Avoid

- Forcing every page into isometric perspective.
- Using screenshots as decorative “proof” without permission or context.
- Tiny labels, excessive 3D surfaces or ambiguous crossing connectors.
- Removing necessary footers, evidence or brand elements for visual purity.

## Best for

System architecture, digital transformation roadmaps, platform strategy, implementation planning and business-technology alignment.
